Dashboards: one page, three places

The same files (web/dash/: one HTML, one CSS, two JS, no build step, no external resources) are served by each classroom Burrow with no login and by the Surface behind the team login. A fourth, smaller page lives on every Pico. Nothing to install: the Pi setup script and the Worker deploy both pick the files up from the repo.

The Wall (/): instant status

One tile per bin, grouped by room then period, sorted by bin number. Fifty tiles render in about a tenth of a second from a 15–40 KB JSON (/api/now), refreshed every 15 s and nudged instantly by the live stream. A filter box and room/period selectors; Projector mode (remembered per browser) hides the filters and enlarges everything for the back of the room. Click a tile for its history.

The header's Skins link opens /wall.html: the seven mission-control takes (Flight Deck, Living Bin, Leaderboard, WORM//NET, Daily Worm, Orbital, Worm Ranch) on this room's live bins. The same page on GitHub Pages shows the whole school through the website's anonymous /api/public/now feed, and falls back to simulated data, clearly labelled, when nothing is reporting.

What the colours mean

StatusRule (any one is enough)
NOMINALnone of the below

ALARMinside air above 30 °C or below 10 °C; any moisture below 15 %
STALEno reading for 3 minutes (the bin has lost its Burrow, or power)

The thresholds live at the top of web/dash/app.js (one small object) if the biology says otherwise. The header shows the counts, one badge per Burrow (room, clock quality ntp/peer/free, cloud ok, castings waiting), the live dot, and the last-update clock.

Bin history (/bin/<uid>): the deep view

Range buttons 6 h / 24 h / 3 d / 7 d / 30 d. Up to three days of 5-minute buckets, hourly beyond that (the server picks; the page says which). One chart per metric family: temperature (air, room, each soil probe), air humidity, soil moisture, air pressure, light, and a small chart for anything else numeric the bin sends. Each line is the bucket average with a shaded min–max band; hover for values and time; min/avg/max for the range in the legend. Gaps stay gaps. All drawn on <canvas> by charts.js, about 300 lines.

Before the first casting seals (15 minutes after a bin's first report) the page says so instead of drawing an empty chart.

Live (/feed): the stream

A ticker of readings as they arrive (team, what changed, by how much) and a sparkline per bin of the last 60 values held in the tab. On a Burrow this is the room's /live server-sent events; on the Surface it is /live/1, /live/2, /live/3 through the three tunnels, each optional. A room whose tunnel is down shows as such and the page keeps polling /api/now instead; it retries the stream with backoff. (The route is /feed because on a Burrow /live is the stream.)

Login and the admin pages (Surface only)

  • Sign in: team from a dropdown grouped by period, the three-word passphrase from the team card, a first name. Staff click "Teacher or admin? Sign in by name" and use name + password. Any page can be bookmarked: a signed-out visitor sees the login form on the same URL and lands back on it afterwards.
  • Admin (link appears for teacher/admin roles): Edges (the three Burrows as the cloud sees them), Who's looking (sessions active in the last 10 minutes, first names and teams), Visits (per-team counts and the recent list), Teams (admin only: list, create, reset passphrase, disable, CSV import; passphrases shown once in a copyable block, and Print cards lays out one card per team), Castings (by room and day, download links), Stats (row counts).
  • On a Burrow none of this exists: /login explains that the room is the credential, and the Admin link is not shown.

The Bin's own page

Unchanged from the worm-bin dashboard everyone already knows (readings, part chips, probes, servo sliders, relay, calibration), plus two things: the status line now reads Dirt Vader (P3 bin 7) live on WormHole · reporting to 192.168.81.10:8080 (or own hotspot, or no Burrow reachable), and a LAST 24 HOURS card draws sparklines from the board's RAM ring (/history, 288 five-minute samples): inside and room air, humidity, both moisture probes, soil probes, light, surface, Wi-Fi signal. It works on the hotspot with no network in the building. The Pico has no clock, so the axis is relative ("6 h") unless a Burrow has told it the time.

Changing the dashboard

  • Edit the files in web/dash/. No build. node --check app.js catches typos.
  • Try it against a real backend on your laptop: the Burrow README "Run it on a laptop" (a Burrow on :8080 serving web/dash, fake bins from simbins.py), or the whole chain with the real Worker: tools/e2e/run.sh starts a local Worker (wrangler, local D1/R2), a Burrow uploading to it, 12 fake bins, and drives the pages in a headless browser: 26 checks, admin flows included.
  • Ship it: on the Surface, npx wrangler deploy (assets go with the Worker); on each Burrow, the unlock → git pull → install.sh → lock dance from the Pi 5 page. The Burrow copy and the cloud copy may differ for a while; nothing breaks.
  • The page detects which host it is on from /api/me (local: true means a Burrow); everything else is the same code.
