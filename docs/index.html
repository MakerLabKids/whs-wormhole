What is this, really?

Wormhole is the hands-on electronics part of Maker Lab Kids at Woodstock High School. You build a little sensor station: a tiny computer (a "Pico"), a sensor, and a small screen, pushed together on a breadboard. You write a short program that tells it what to measure (the temperature of a worm bin, how wet the soil is, how much light gets in) and it shows the answer on its screen and on any phone in the room.

Everything runs in a web browser (nothing to install on the computer), and each station makes its own WiFi, so it works even with no internet. This site walks you through it, one small step at a time. You are not expected to already know any of this.
