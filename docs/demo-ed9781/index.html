Demo The Wrigglers bin

One bin, one phone, about ten minutes. No router or Raspberry Pi needed: the bin makes its own Wi-Fi.

1 Bring

  • The Wrigglers bin, sensors and fan attached
  • A USB power source: a phone charger or power bank, plus its micro-USB cable
  • Your phone (or a laptop) to show the bin's page
  • Optional: a laptop with internet for the Mission Control demo
  • Sponsor one-sheets and brochures to hand out

2 Set up

  1. Plug the bin into power. Its screen plays the power-on show and the fan runs for 10 seconds: that's the fan test.
  2. After about 30 seconds, join the Wi-Fi The Wrigglers on your phone. If the phone says "no internet", choose to stay connected.
  3. Open http://192.168.4.38. Readings update every second.

Do I need an Opal or a hotspot?

No. The bin's own Wi-Fi is all this demo needs. A phone hotspot doesn't help: the bin sends its readings to a classroom Raspberry Pi, not straight to the internet. Seeing this bin live on whs.makerlabkids.com needs a classroom Opal router and Pi with internet.

If something's off

No "The Wrigglers" Wi-Fi? Wait a minute: if the bin finds a classroom network first, it joins that instead, and its screen shows where. Unplug and replug to start over. A reading shows "--"? That sensor's wire is loose; the rest keep working. Don't tap Rename: it changes the Wi-Fi name and the PIN.

3 Show it off

  1. Breathe on it. Breathe on the air sensor inside the lid. Humidity jumps on the phone within seconds. "Worms breathe through their skin, so damp air matters."
  2. Warm a probe. Hold a soil temperature probe in your fist. Watch the temperature climb.
  3. Wave at the laser. Slide a hand under the laser ranger and watch the distance change, in millimeters. "In the bin it watches the bedding level drop as the worms eat."
  4. Write on the screen. On the page, open Messages on the screen, enter the PIN 1126 and your first name, and type a short message (16 letters a line). It shows on the bin's screen after the current show. Try a visitor's name: "Sponsors can see their name here."
  5. Make the fan kick on. In Settings (PIN 1126), set the fan humidity to 30 % and the check to every 1 minute, then Restart to apply. The fan starts within a minute. Afterward, tap Put them back.
  6. Show the day. Last 24 hours charts what the bin has seen today.
  7. Mission Control. On a laptop with internet, open makerlabkids.github.io/whs-wormhole/demos/mission-control.html: seven ways to watch a classroom full of bins, with pretend data.

The website

Anyone can view whs.makerlabkids.com. Each teacher has her own admin login (given separately). Signing in adds messages to any bin's screen from anywhere, plus the admin pages. That works for bins that report through a classroom Pi.
