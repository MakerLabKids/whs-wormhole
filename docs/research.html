Project Ideas

We know some students and teachers aren't yet familiar with what's possible using the tools and techniques we've shared. This page shows some of the projects you can build with what we've taught. We've grouped them to give a sense of how involved each one is, and every card lists the gear it needs (and flags when you might borrow a part from another group).

How to read a card

Starter: one sensor, one session, clear result Basic: a real experiment with a control Advanced: multi-day or multi-sensor, more moving parts Next Level: marquee build, show it off (only 5 of these) Meta: improve our own setup

Horse-trade flag: the orange box means the project needs more of something than one bin normally gets (extra temp probes, the shared geophones, a spare Pico), so you'll be borrowing or swapping with another group. Plan it with them and your teacher.
